Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to make custom grillz?

+

Custom grillz typically take 2-3 weeks to complete from the time we receive your impressions. This includes design consultation, crafting, and quality control. Rush orders may be available for an additional fee.

What materials do you use?

+

We use only the highest quality materials including 10K, 14K, and 18K gold, sterling silver, and premium CZ stones. All materials are safe for oral use and hypoallergenic.

How do I get dental impressions?

+

We provide a dental impression kit with your order. Simply follow the included instructions, or you can visit your local dentist for professional impressions. We also offer virtual consultations.

What's your return policy?

+

Since all grillz are custom-made to your specifications, we don't accept returns unless there's a manufacturing defect. We offer free adjustments within 30 days if the fit isn't perfect.

Do you ship internationally?

+

Yes, we ship worldwide! International shipping typically takes 5-10 business days. Customs duties and taxes may apply depending on your country's regulations.

How much do custom grillz cost?

+

Prices vary depending on materials, design complexity, and number of teeth. Although we do offer an online price list, contact us for a personalized quote.

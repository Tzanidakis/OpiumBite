Scroll through the making of an Opium piece

Digital
measurements

Every piece starts with a precise impression, captured through 3D scanning. We digitize the shape of your teeth to create the foundation for a personal fit.

Digital
genesis

We sculpt the design tooth by tooth, then 3D-print it in wax and algin. This wax model brings the digital piece into physical form, ready for casting in your chosen precious metal.

Specialized
casting

The fire. Silver or gold is melted and forced into the mold under pressure. No bubbles, no shortcuts—only the shape we designed.

Finishing

The hand. Filed, buffed, polished and set by hand. It only leaves the atelier when it looks inevitable.

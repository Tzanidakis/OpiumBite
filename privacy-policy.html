Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Who handles your information

Stelios Chatzidakis, trading as Opium Bite, is responsible for the personal information described in this policy. We are based in Heraklion, Crete, Greece. For privacy requests, contact opiumbite@gmail.com or +30 698 507 1700.

Information we receive and why

When you contact us, we receive the name, email, phone number, message, selected product details and files you submit. We use these details to answer your enquiry, discuss a design and prepare a quote. This processing supports steps you request before a contract and, if you place an order, performance of that contract under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. The form identifies the fields needed to submit an enquiry. You can also contact us directly by email or phone.

Hosting and security services may process IP addresses, browser and device information, request times and technical logs to deliver and protect the site. Our legitimate interests in operating a reliable, secure website form the basis for this processing under Article 6(1)(f). We may retain transaction records where required by tax or other legal obligations under Article 6(1)(c).

Please limit attachments to the information needed for your design enquiry. If dental impressions or other information concerning health are needed to fulfil a custom order, we will explain the purpose and applicable legal basis before arranging their collection.

Services that receive information

Cloudflare provides website hosting, media delivery and security. Contact-form messages and attachments are submitted through FormSubmit and delivered to our Gmail inbox. Google Fonts supplies the website fonts and receives the connection information needed to serve them. FormSubmit may present a Google reCAPTCHA check when you submit the form. These services process information needed to provide their functions.

Some service providers operate internationally and may process information outside the European Economic Area. Where GDPR requires safeguards for a transfer we make, the transfer must use an applicable adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses. Contact us for information about safeguards relevant to your enquiry. Provider information is available in the Cloudflare privacy policy, FormSubmit privacy policy and Google privacy policy.

Browser storage and cookies

The site stores your language and cookie preference in local storage. It uses session storage for the introductory animation and to restore the page when you return to a tab. Local storage remains until cleared or replaced. Session storage normally lasts for the browser tab session. You can clear this storage in your browser settings and reopen the preference panel through Cookie preferences in the footer.

The current website includes no optional analytics or advertising tags. Hosting, security and the external form or CAPTCHA service may use storage needed for their functions. External Instagram links open a separate service governed by its own privacy policy.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to respond, handle follow-up discussions and resolve related issues. If an enquiry becomes an order, relevant records are retained for fulfilment, applicable legal recordkeeping obligations and the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. The type of record, the relationship with you and applicable statutory periods determine retention. You can ask us about the retention period that applies to your information.

Your rights

Subject to the conditions in GDPR, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction and portability of your information. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests. If we rely on consent for a particular use, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

Contact opiumbite@gmail.com to exercise these rights. You can lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority or another competent supervisory authority. We respond within the time limits set by GDPR.

Updates

This policy describes the current website and contact process. We will update it when those activities change and show the revision date on this page.

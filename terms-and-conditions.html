Enquiries and quotations

The website displays custom grillz and jewelry and allows you to request a quote. Submitting an enquiry starts a discussion with the atelier. An order is agreed when we confirm the design, materials, size, total price, payment arrangements and delivery terms with you in writing and you accept them.

Displayed prices are starting prices in euros. The final quote depends on the selected design, metal, karat, number of teeth and any stones or other custom work. Before you commit, we will confirm the full amount payable, including applicable taxes and delivery charges, and explain any import charges that may be payable separately. Any deposit, payment schedule or cancellation charge must be disclosed and agreed before payment.

Product photographs illustrate the design. Colour can vary with lighting, screen settings, finish and gold purity. Confirm your chosen metal, karat, design, dimensions and fit requirements in the written order details. Final specifications and any production or delivery estimate will be agreed for your individual order.

Production and delivery

Production starts according to the agreed order arrangements, including any required impressions, measurements and payment. Tell us promptly if your details or requested specifications change. Changes may affect price and timing and require written agreement. We will give you the applicable delivery arrangements and address any delay with you.

Cancellation and withdrawal

Contact us promptly if you want to change or cancel an order. For eligible distance purchases, consumers normally have 14 days from delivery to exercise the statutory right of withdrawal. The statutory exception for goods made to the consumer’s specifications or clearly personalised may apply to custom pieces. We will identify the applicable withdrawal terms before an order is agreed. A standard item is not automatically personalised solely because it is produced after ordering.

Any applicable withdrawal instructions, return arrangements and return costs will be supplied before the contract is concluded. Statutory remedies for goods that are faulty or do not match the agreed specifications continue to apply, including for personalised goods.

Faults, fit and complaints

If a piece arrives damaged, appears faulty or differs from your agreed order, contact us with your order details and a description of the problem. We will assess it and arrange the appropriate remedy under applicable consumer law. Wearing and caring for grillz

Grillz are decorative jewelry. Follow the care and fitting guidance supplied with your piece. Ask a qualified dental professional about suitability if you have dental conditions or concerns. Stop wearing a piece and seek professional advice if you experience pain, irritation or an adverse reaction.
